# U.S. top-100 city hotline review — 2026-08-12

## Method

- Ranking: U.S. Census Bureau July 1, 2025 incorporated-place estimates (`POPESTIMATE2025`), descending population.
- Locality coverage: canonical geography matched against each place and intersecting county using 2025 Census TIGER/Line boundaries.
- Research acceptance: a distinct local or county-scoped hotline, current contact number, and service scope had to be confirmed on a live government or first-party provider page.
- Excluded: national 911/988 duplicates, state-only routing, directories/search snippets, facility switchboards, non-hotline programs, unclear geography, and pages that did not establish the claimed contact.
- A `reviewed_no_candidate` result does not assert that no local help exists; it means no distinct record met the promotion threshold in this bounded pass.
