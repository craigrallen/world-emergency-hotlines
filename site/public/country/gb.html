🧒 Child protection

Childline

Curated
📞 0800 1111
⏰ 7.30am to 3.30am daily (phone and 1-to-1 chat)🗣️ English💰 Free
💬 Live chat ↗🌐 Website ↗

For: anyone under 19 in the UK

Free, private and confidential service for anyone under 19 in the UK. Call 0800 1111 (the call won't show up on your phone bill). Other ways to get support via childline.org.uk: (1) 1-to-1 counsellor chat — typed conversation with a Childline counsellor. (2) Message boards for peer support — moderated by Childline. (3) Ask Sam — email a Childline advice columnist. (4) The Toolbox — games, coping activities, the Calm Zone breathing exercises, video diaries. (5) Topic pages covering abuse, bullying, mental health, exams, family, relationships and sex, LGBTQ+, your body. Also available via the 'For Me' mobile app for discreet, encrypted access.
